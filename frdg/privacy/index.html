FRDG privacy policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

FRDG is a small game about opening a fridge. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information.

  • No accounts of ours. On Android the game can use Google Play Games, Google's own gaming profile, if you have it. Google signs you in; we never see your name, email or password.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking. The game contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking code.
  • No servers of ours. The game never sends anything to us or to anyone else, apart from the Google Play features below.
  • Your save. Your fridge, Fridge Log and stats are saved in a file on your device. If you're signed in to Google Play Games, a copy is also kept in your Google account (Play Games "Saved Games") so it survives a new phone or a reinstall, and the awards you earn show up as Play Games achievements. That copy is stored by Google, only the game can read it on your behalf, and we have no access to it. Google Play Games also collects its own diagnostics, under Google's privacy policy. You can delete your Play Games data from your Play Games profile or your Google account settings.
  • Snapshots are images saved on your device. They only leave it if you choose to share one, through your phone's own share menu.
  • Purchases (the optional Deluxe Fridge) are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details; Google tells the game only that the purchase happened.
  • Reminders are optional, off by default, and scheduled on your device.

Children. FRDG follows the Google Play Families Policy. It has no ads and collects nothing for us, from anyone, including children. The only data that leaves the device is what Google Play Games keeps in a signed-in player's own Google account, as described above.

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes. If this policy changes, this page changes, and the date at the top with it.
