Accessibility

Last updated: November 12, 2026

We want this site and our games to work for everyone who wants to stare into a fridge. We're a very small studio, so we fix things as we find them, and we'd rather hear about a problem than not.

This website

We aim for WCAG 2.2 level AA. The pages are plain HTML: they work with screen readers, keyboard navigation, browser zoom and text resizing, light and dark mode, and they have no pop-ups, cookies, autoplaying media or time limits.

FRDG

What the game does today:

  • No time pressure. No fail states, no timers that punish you, nothing you can miss by being slow. A visit can take as long as you like.
  • Settings on the "?" page: sound on or off, vibration on or off, less motion (no door swing, light flicker, rattling drawers or pulsing sparkles; it starts on if your phone asks apps to remove animations), and slower pop-ups.
  • No flashing. Nothing in the game flashes three or more times in a second.
  • Readable text. Large, high-contrast pixel letters, scaled up to fit your screen. Text colors meet the WCAG AA contrast ratio of 4.5:1 or better.
  • Nothing by sound or color alone. Every sound comes with something on screen, and new things are marked with a shape and a count, not only a color.
  • One hand, portrait. Everything is a tap, a hold or a short swipe.

What doesn't work well yet:

  • Screen readers. Buttons and menus have labels, but the fridge itself is pixel art, and screen readers can't describe what's on the shelves yet. How much works also depends on your phone and on the game engine's support.
  • Text size follows your screen, not your phone's font-size setting.
  • Holding and dragging is the only way to move items and magnets.

Tell us

If something on this site or in FRDG is hard to use, or you need it some other way, email info@cloudsnorkel.com. Tell us what you were trying to do and what got in the way. We read everything and aim to reply within a week.
